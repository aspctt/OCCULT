# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

## [1.4.4] - 2026-09-30

### Changed

- Needs KSP 1.9.0 or later, up from 1.8.0, and supports every release up to 1.12.x

### Fixed

- A stray `{}` in Remove Stock EL Parts

## [1.4.3] - 2024-12-17

### Added

- AJE Capitalization (experimental): capitalizes and reformats AJE part titles
- Remove Stock EL Parts: removes the stock parts from Extraplanetary Launchpads

### Changed

- RR Structural is optional in PatchManager instead of always on

## [1.4.2] - 2024-12-06

### Fixed

- Rational Resources configs missing from the AirplanePlus tail connectors

## [1.4.1] - 2024-12-06

### Fixed

- Parts missing from the AirplanePlus VAB Organizer patch

## [1.4.0] - 2024-12-06

### Added

- Fueled APP Wings: fuel tanks and the Rational Resources fuel switch for AirplanePlus wings
- VAB Organizer patches for KW Rocketry, AirplanePlus and OPT Spaceplane

### Changed

- Minor refactors

### Fixed

- Fuel values for one of the wings in Fueled Stock Wings

## [1.3.0] - 2024-12-01

### Added

- Jool LqdHydrogen Buff: more LqdHydrogen to collect from Jool's atmosphere and exosphere

## [1.2.2] - 2024-11-26

### Fixed

- A typo in RR Structural

## [1.2.1] - 2024-11-26

### Added

- RR Structural, always on: the Rational Resources structural option on every part missing it

### Changed

- The RR patches no longer add a structural option by hand, RR Structural covers it

## [1.2.0] - 2024-11-25

### Added

- Fueled Stock Wings: fuel tanks and the Rational Resources fuel switch for stock wings
- Mk3 Expansion - RR and NF Aeronautics - RR: the Rational Resources fuel switch for some Mk3 Expansion and Near Future Aeronautics parts
- A VAB Organizer patch for Mk3 Expansion

## [1.1.1] - 2024-11-23

### Removed

- MK3 Rebalance, added by mistake

## [1.1.0] - 2024-11-21

### Added

- MK2 Rebalance: lower mass for stock MK2 parts
- MK2 Expansion Rebalance: lower mass for Mk2 Expansion parts and better stats for some of its engines
- MK3 Rebalance

## [1.0.1] - 2024-11-18

### Fixed

- A naming mistake

## [1.0.0] - 2024-11-17

First release.

### Added

- 4x Robotics Torque: quadrupled torque for robotic parts
- JX2 Antenna Feed: the Near Future Launch Vehicles antenna feed on JX2 antennas
- SystemHeat Nuclear Responsiveness: instant throttle response for SystemHeat fission engines
- Jool Glow on Laythe: a little glow from Jool on Laythe
